# StudyBuddy

Spelling and study games for the family: flash cards, dictation and arcade games, with a natural reading voice.
You can also record your own voice for the words and the alphabet.

## Install

**[⬇ Download StudyBuddy](../../releases/latest)**, then pick the file for your computer:

| Your computer | Download |
| --- | --- |
| Mac | **StudyBuddy-Mac.pkg** |
| Windows PC | **StudyBuddy-Setup.exe** |

That's the only download. The reading voice and everything else are inside. You don't need the internet once it's installed.

### Mac

1. Open the file you downloaded. A message says Apple can't check it, because StudyBuddy isn't from the App Store.
   - **macOS 15 Sequoia or newer:** click **Done**. Then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down,
     and click **Open Anyway** next to StudyBuddy-Mac.pkg. Enter your Mac's password if it asks.
   - **Older macOS:** right-click (or Control-click) the file, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
2. Click **Continue** and **Install** through the installer.
3. StudyBuddy is now in your **Applications** folder. The first time it starts, it may take a moment.

### Windows

1. Open the file you downloaded.
2. If a blue box says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the installer. StudyBuddy gets a desktop shortcut and a Start menu entry.

## Good to know

- To record your voice, the computer asks to use the microphone the first time. Say **OK**.
- Reading a **photo** of a word list works on Windows and on newer Macs (M1, M2, M3…).
  On older Intel Macs, type the list in instead.
